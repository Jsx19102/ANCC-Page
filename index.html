One agreement

One Agreement. Three Names.

USMCA
United States
United States – Mexico – Canada Agreement
The legal name in U.S. federal law and in cross-border filings originating from the United States.
CUSMA
Canada
Canada – United States – Mexico Agreement
The official designation in Canadian statute, regulations and bilingual federal communications.
T-MEC
Mexico
Tratado entre México, Estados Unidos y Canadá
The Spanish-language designation used by the Mexican government, customs and the private sector.

Same legal text. Ratified under three national names. $1.86 trillion in annual trade. ANCC covers all three — plus NAFTA history since 1994.
